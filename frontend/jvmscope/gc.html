Interpretation & capture coverage
Pauses are distinct from concurrent phases and allocation stalls. Pause share covers the first-to-last observed event, not the entire JVM lifetime or application throughput. Heap samples are reported observations; connecting lines do not measure allocation rate. Missing fields remain unknown. Review annotations are evidence and configurable heuristics, not a leak diagnosis.
